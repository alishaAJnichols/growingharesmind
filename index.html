After the race

The hare lost to the tortoise — everyone remembers that part. Fewer people know what happened after.

He went back the next day, and the day after that. His grandmother told him he didn't need to beat anyone next time — he just needed to keep learning. So he stopped racing others and started chasing something of his own: a degree in aeronautical engineering, on a campus with nice grass.

He stopped taking so many breaks. And he got there.
